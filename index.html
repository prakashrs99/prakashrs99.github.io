I am a chemist working in synthetic methodology, photoredox catalysis, and functionalization of organic molecules. My doctoral research at the University of Antwerp focused on photoredox catalysis and late-stage functionalization using homogeneous and heterogeneous photocatalytic systems. I am currently a Postdoctoral Fellow at the University of Alberta, where my research includes nucleoside chemistry and C–H bond functionalization of nucleoside analogues for medicinal-chemistry applications. I am interested in research strategies that combine mechanistic insight, experimental practicality, and more sustainable approaches to molecular synthesis.
